Getting started

Which Apple Health permissions should I allow?

For the recovery call, allow Sleep, Heart Rate Variability and Resting Heart Rate. For body tracking, allow Weight, Body Fat Percentage and Height. Date of birth and biological sex are only used for the metabolic age estimate.

To change them later: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → STDY.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

For the recovery call, yes, or another device that writes sleep, HRV and resting heart rate to Apple Health. Without one you can still log workouts, follow your plan and track your body.

Do I need an account?

No. STDY has no sign-up. Your data syncs through your own iCloud.

Recovery

My recovery says "No data" or "Calibrating"

Recovery compares this morning's HRV and resting heart rate with your own 30-day average, so it needs a few nights of data first. It shows as calibrating until it has at least a week.

If it says no data, wear your watch to bed, make sure Sleep tracking is on in the Health app, and check that STDY has permission to read Sleep, HRV and Resting Heart Rate.

Sleep is missing. Does that lower my score?

No. A missing reading is left out and the other two are re-weighted. Days without data are never counted as zero.

How is the score worked out?

Sleep counts for 40%, HRV for 35% and resting heart rate for 25%. The total puts you in one of four states: Ready to Crush (85–100), Good to Go (65–84), Manage It (40–64) or Recover (0–39).

Strength and body

How are sets per muscle counted?

Every working set counts fully toward the exercise's main muscle. Compound lifts also count half a set for the muscles that help: presses for triceps, rows and pull-ups for biceps, squats and lunges for glutes, deadlifts for back. If you have an active plan, the target is the sets your plan prescribes; otherwise it's a weekly minimum based on your experience level.

Why doesn't my weight match the scale exactly?

The big number is your latest reading. The trend and weekly rate are smoothed over 7 to 28 days so that water and food swings don't mislead you.

How is metabolic age estimated?

STDY compares the calories your lean mass burns at rest with what's typical for someone of your age, height and weight. It's an estimate for tracking change over time, not a medical measure.

Widget and notifications

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for STDY, and pick the small or medium size. The widget updates whenever you open the app.

The widget shows old information

Open STDY once to refresh it. If it still looks out of date, remove the widget and add it again.

How do I turn off the weekly summary?

In STDY, go to More and switch off Weekly summary. You can also manage all notifications in Settings → Notifications → STDY.

Sync, purchases and data

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on the new phone and install STDY. Your log, plan and photos sync automatically. Make sure iCloud is on for STDY in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

How does the free trial work?

Every feature is free for 7 days. After that, a one-time purchase of $14.99 unlocks STDY for good. There's no subscription and nothing renews.

I bought STDY but it's asking me to pay again

Go to More → Restore Purchase, using the same Apple ID you bought with. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so they carry over to new devices.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove everything on your iPhone. To remove synced data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → STDY. See the privacy policy for details.
