Recovery

One call each morning

Your HRV and resting heart rate are compared with your own 30-day average, not population norms. Sleep is scored against the hours you actually got. Missing data is left out, never counted against you.

85–100

Ready to Crush

Every signal is up. Go heavy, chase a top set.

65–84

Good to Go

Recovery is solid. Train as planned.

40–64

Manage It

Some signals are down. Hold your weights and keep 2–3 reps in reserve.

0–39

Recover

Your body is behind. Walk, stretch, or cut volume in half.

Weighting: sleep 40%, HRV 35%, resting heart rate 25%.

Body

Read the trend, not the scale

Daily weigh-ins bounce around. STDY smooths them into a 7-day trend and a weekly rate, pulls body fat from your smart scale through Apple Health, and turns a target body fat into a goal weight that keeps your lean mass.

  • Lean mass and fat massSplit out from every reading that has body fat.
  • Pace checkA heads-up if you're losing more than 1% of body weight a week, the point where muscle starts to go.
  • Progress photosSide by side, stored with the rest of your data.
