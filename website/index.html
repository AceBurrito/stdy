Recovery

The morning call

STDY looks at last night's sleep, your HRV and your resting heart rate, and compares them with your own last 30 days. A 41 ms HRV is great for some people and a bad night for others, so it only ever compares you with you. If your watch didn't catch your sleep, that night is skipped. It won't count as zero.

Strength

Sets per muscle, against your actual plan

Most volume trackers hold you to a generic number. STDY holds you to the program you're running, and tells you when that program is too light for a muscle to grow. A row counts half a set for biceps and a bench press counts half for triceps, because they're doing work too.

  • Last time is right thereEach exercise shows what you lifted last session and what to try today, so you're not scrolling back through history between sets.
  • Progress per liftEstimated 1RM, top set and volume over time. New bests get marked when they happen.

Body

The scale is a little wrong every day

Water, salt, a late dinner. Your weight swings a couple of pounds for reasons that have nothing to do with fat, which makes a single weigh-in close to useless. STDY smooths it into a 7-day trend so you can see which way you're actually heading.

  • Got a smart scale?If it writes body fat to Apple Health, STDY splits out lean mass and turns a body fat goal into a weight to aim for.
  • A nudge if you're cutting too fastLosing more than about 1% of your body weight a week usually means losing muscle too. STDY will tell you.
Privacy

There's no server

I didn't build one. There's no account to make, your log stays on your phone and syncs through your own iCloud, and health data never leaves Apple Health. There are no ads or analytics in the app, so there's nothing for me to sell and nobody to sell it to.
