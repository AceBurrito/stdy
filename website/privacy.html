Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

The short version
  • STDY has no account, no server and no analytics. We don't collect your data.
  • Your training log is stored on your device and synced through your own private iCloud.
  • Health readings stay in Apple Health. STDY reads them on your device to show you your numbers.
  • We never sell, share or use your health data for advertising.

Who we are

STDY is made by [Developer or company name] ("we", "us"). This policy covers the STDY iPhone app, its widgets, and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. STDY doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else. The app makes no network requests to our own servers or to third-party services, and it contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code.

Apple Health

STDY asks for permission before it reads any Health data, and you choose exactly which types to share in the Health app. You can change this at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → STDY. Data read from Apple Health is used only to show you your own information in the app. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, STDY syncs your log, plan and photos through Apple's CloudKit, using the private database in your own iCloud account. Apple stores that data under its own privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can turn sync off in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Camera, photos and Face ID

The camera is used only when you take a progress photo. STDY saves to your photo library only when you ask it to. If you turn on Face ID protection, Apple handles the face match on your device; STDY only receives a yes or no.

Notifications

Rest timer alerts and the weekly summary are local notifications, created on your iPhone from data already on it. No notification content passes through a server.

Purchases

Payments and free trials are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple only tells the app whether you have access.

Deleting your data

  • Delete the STDY app to remove everything stored on your iPhone.
  • To remove synced data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, choose STDY, and delete its data.
  • Data STDY wrote to Apple Health (logged workouts) can be removed in the Health app.

Children

STDY is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
